    Audit of convertlens.com: what I found, and what to fix

    Checked on Oct 9, 2026. In the browser I read the homepage links, Events and Contact pages. I checked the HTTP status of 40 URLs with curl. The remaining product pages were read with an automated page reader, so treat their wording as a close summary.

    Findings, highest impact first

    #ImpactWhat I foundWhereFix in this prototype

    Suggested site structure

    Header (5 items)Platform ▾ · Solutions ▾ · Integrations ▾ · Resources ▾ · Company ▾, plus Search, phone and one “Book a demo” button.
    Platform = products + tools11 pages in three groups: Measure, Capture & convert leads, AI tools. One “Products & tools” overview with filters, so nothing is hidden.
    Events where visitors expect themResources dropdown, a strip on the homepage, and the footer. Each event gets date, city, booth and a “Book a meeting” button.
    One booking pathEvery “Book a demo” goes to /book-demo. “Talk to us” goes to /contact. No more “Get Started” that opens the same form.
